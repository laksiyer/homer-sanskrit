वकदाल्भ्यकथा तथा क्रूसेसस्य प्रार्थना

Iliad 1.8–100 · Kāṭhaka Saṃhitā 10.6 · Mahābhārata Śalyaparvan

Comparative status: Narrative and ritual parallel, not an established linguistic cognate, direct borrowing, or an identification of Apollo with Rudra.

Narrative comparison

HomerKāṭhaka / Mahābhārata

Kāṭhaka Saṃhitā 10.6

सोऽग्नये रुद्रवतेऽष्टाकपालं निरवपत् कृष्णानां व्रीहीणाम् ।

Vaka offers an eight-cake oblation of black rice to Agni Rudravat.

अग्निर्वै रुद्रो रुद्रायैवैनमपिदधाति ।

The ritual prose identifies Agni with Rudra in explaining the destructive rite. The subsequent restorative offering is to Agni Surabhimat, with white rice.

Mahābhārata · Śalyaparvan

स तैरेव जुहावाग्नौ राष्ट्रं मांसैर्महातपाः ॥

The epic version has Vaka offering the kingdom into fire with pieces of dead cattle. This differs in ritual details from the Kāṭhaka black-rice offering.

ततो विप्रादिकाः प्राहुः पशुविप्रकृतस्त्वया ।
मांसैरभिजुहोतीदं तव राष्ट्रं मुनिर्बकः ॥

The advisers tell the king that his conduct toward the priest concerning the cattle brought about the calamity.

Interpretation

The shared sequence is insult to a ritual specialist, appeal or ritual retaliation, damage to the ruler's realm, discovery of the cause, and reconciliation. It offers an unusually close comparative narrative, without proving a shared Indo-European original or direct borrowing.
