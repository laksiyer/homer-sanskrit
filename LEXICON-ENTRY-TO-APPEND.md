## Χρύσης (Khrusēs) → क्रूसेसः

**Status:** Narrative and ritual comparison; not a lexical cognate
**Lines:** 1.11, 1.22, 1.33, 1.35, 1.42, 1.43, 1.50, 1.92, 1.100
**Forms:** क्रूसेसः, क्रूसेसं, क्रूसेसस्य

Chryses' insult by Agamemnon, invocation of Apollo, punishment of the army, and subsequent expiation recall the story of Vaka Dālbhi and Dhṛtarāṣṭra Vaicitravīrya in the Kāṭhaka Saṃhitā 10.6. Vaka offers black rice to Agni Rudravat, and a restorative Agni Surabhimat offering follows. A later Mahābhārata account (Śalyaparvan 9.42 in one numbering) presents an expanded narrative with a different ritual medium. Neither source proves that Apollo and Rudra are identical, or that either tradition directly borrowed from the other.
