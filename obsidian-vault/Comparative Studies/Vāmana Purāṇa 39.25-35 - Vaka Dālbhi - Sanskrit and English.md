# Vāmana Purāṇa 39.25–35: Vaka Dālbhi | Sanskrit and English

**वकदाल्भ्यकथा · वामनपुराणम् ३९.२५–३५**

**Primary source:** [Read Sanskrit original](https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z200925200444/view)

> Editorial method: the Sanskrit below follows the cited online recension, with sentence spacing added where useful. The English is a new close translation prepared for this study, not an existing published translation.

**Scope:** Vāmana Purāṇa 39.25–35 (another numbering: Sāromāhātmya 18.25–35). Verse 29 contains compressed wording requiring further textual collation.

## Verse 25

**Sanskrit**

तत्रैव तीर्थं विख्यातमवकीर्णेति नामतः ।

**English**

There too is a celebrated sacred bathing-place known by the name Avakīrṇa.

## Verse 26

**Sanskrit**

यस्मिन् तीर्थे बको दाल्भ्यो धृतराष्ट्रममर्षणम् । जुहाव वाहनैः सार्धं तत्राबुध्यत् ततो नृपः ॥

**English**

At that tīrtha, Baka Dālbhi offered the wrathful Dhṛtarāṣṭra, together with his vehicles, into the sacrificial fire; the king then came to understand what had happened.

## Verse 27

**Sanskrit**

ऋषय ऊचुः । कथं प्रतिष्ठितं तीर्थमवकीर्णेति नामतः । धृतराष्ट्रेण राज्ञा च स किमर्थं प्रसादितः ॥

**English**

The sages asked: “How did the sacred place called Avakīrṇa become established, and why did King Dhṛtarāṣṭra seek to appease him?”

## Verse 28

**Sanskrit**

लोमहर्षण उवाच । ऋषयो नैमिषेया ये दक्षिणार्थं ययुः पुरा । तत्रैव च बको दाल्भ्यो धृतराष्ट्रमयाचत ॥

**English**

Lomaharṣaṇa said: “Long ago the Naimiṣeya sages went in search of sacrificial gifts. At that time Baka Dālbhi requested such a gift from Dhṛtarāṣṭra.”

## Verse 29

**Sanskrit**

तेनापि तत्र निन्दार्थमुक्तं पश्वनृतं तु यत् । ततः क्रोधेन महता मांसमुत्कृत्य तत्र ह ॥

**English**

The king spoke contemptuously to him concerning the animals, in words described by the text as false or abusive. Baka, filled with great anger, cut off flesh there.

## Verse 30

**Sanskrit**

पृथूदके महातीर्थे अवकीर्णेति नामतः । जुहाव धृतराष्ट्रस्य राष्ट्रं नरपतेस्ततः ॥

**English**

At the great sacred place called Avakīrṇa at Pṛthūdaka, he then offered King Dhṛtarāṣṭra’s realm into the sacrificial fire.

## Verse 31

**Sanskrit**

हूयमाने तदा राष्ट्रे प्रवृत्ते यज्ञकर्मणि । अक्षियत ततो राष्ट्रं नृपतेर्दुष्कृतेन वै ॥

**English**

As the sacrificial rite proceeded and the kingdom was being offered, the king’s realm began to waste away because of the ruler’s wrongful deed.

## Verse 32

**Sanskrit**

ततः स चिन्तयामास ब्राह्मणस्य विचेष्टितम् । पुरोहितेन संयुक्तो रत्नान्यादाय सर्वशः ॥

**English**

Then the king considered the brahmin’s action. Accompanied by his priest, he gathered valuables of every kind.

## Verse 33

**Sanskrit**

प्रसादनार्थं विप्रस्य ह्यवकीर्णं ययौ तदा । प्रसादितः स राज्ञा च तुष्टः प्रोवाच तं नृपम् ॥

**English**

He went to Avakīrṇa to win the brahmin’s favor. Appeased by the king and satisfied, Baka spoke to him.

## Verse 34

**Sanskrit**

ब्राह्मणा नावमन्तव्याः पुरुषेण विजानता । अवज्ञातो ब्राह्मणस्तु हन्यात् त्रिपुरुषं कुलम् ॥

**English**

“A discerning person must not treat brahmins with contempt. A brahmin who is despised may destroy a family for three generations.”

## Verse 35

**Sanskrit**

एवमुक्त्वा स नृपतिं राज्येन यशसा पुनः । उत्थापयामास ततस्तस्य राज्ञो हिते स्थितः ॥

**English**

Having spoken thus, he restored the king to his kingdom and renown, thereafter acting for that ruler’s welfare.

## Comparative reading
