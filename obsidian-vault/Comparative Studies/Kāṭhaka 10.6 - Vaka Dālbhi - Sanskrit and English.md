# Kāṭhaka Saṃhitā 10.6: Vaka Dālbhi | Sanskrit and English

**वकदाल्भ्यकथा · काठकसंहिता १०.६**

**Primary source:** [Read Sanskrit original](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/काठकसंहिता_(विस्वरः)/स्थानकम्_१०)

> Editorial method: the Sanskrit below follows the cited online recension, with sentence spacing added where useful. The English is a new close translation prepared for this study, not an existing published translation.

**Scope:** The complete Vaka Dālbhi narrative and its immediately following explanation of the Agni Rudravat and Agni Surabhimat offerings within KS 10.6. The other rites in section 10.6 are outside this narrative.

**Textual note:** The stretch involving the meat and its preparation is syntactically compressed in the online text; the English marks the ambiguity rather than inventing a precise reading.

## Passage 1

**Sanskrit**

नैमिष्या वै सत्रमासत । त उत्थाय सप्तविंशतिं कुरुपञ्चालेषु वत्सतरानवन्वत ।

**English**

The Naimiṣeya sages were engaged in a sacrificial session. When it concluded, they went among the Kuru-Pañcālas seeking twenty-seven young bulls.

## Passage 2

**Sanskrit**

तान् वको दाल्भिरब्रवीत् — यूयमेवैतान् विभजध्वम् । इममहं धृतराष्ट्रं वैचित्रवीर्यं गमिष्यामि । स मह्यं गृहान् करिष्यतीति ।

**English**

Vaka Dālbhi said to them: “You divide these among yourselves. I shall go to Dhṛtarāṣṭra Vaicitravīrya; he will arrange hospitality and gifts for me.”

## Passage 3

**Sanskrit**

तमागच्छत् । तन्नासूर्क्षत् । तं प्राकालयत् । एता गा ब्रह्मबन्ध इत्यब्रवीत् पशुपतिर्गा हन्ति । ताः परः पचमानश्चरेति ।

**English**

He approached the king, but the king did not receive him as he had expected. He drove him away, saying, “Here are cattle, you mere kinsman of brahmins: Paśupati kills cattle. Go off and cook those!”

## Passage 4

**Sanskrit**

तासां देवसूर्मे राजान्नं प्रासुपोदिति सक्थान्युत्कर्तमपचत् । तस्मिन् पचमाने व्युदस्यत् ।

**English**

Vaka took portions from those animals and began cooking them. This part of the transmitted prose is compressed and its wording difficult; the exact force of the intervening utterance requires consultation of a printed edition and commentary.

## Passage 5

**Sanskrit**

सोऽग्नये रुद्रवतेऽष्टाकपालं निरवपत् कृष्णानां व्रीहीणाम् ।

**English**

He prepared an eight-portion sacrificial cake of black rice for Agni Rudravat, Agni in his Rudra-associated form.

## Passage 6

**Sanskrit**

तस्य यत् किंच धृतराष्ट्रस्यासीत् तत् सर्वमवकर्णं विद्राणमभिव्यौच्छत् ।

**English**

All that belonged to Dhṛtarāṣṭra fell into disarray and ruin.

## Passage 7

**Sanskrit**

ता विप्रश्निका अविन्दन् — ब्राह्मणो वै त्वायमभिचरति, तस्मिनाथस्वेति ।

**English**

The diviners discerned the cause and told him: “This brahmin is performing a hostile rite against you. Seek his protection and favor.”

## Passage 8

**Sanskrit**

तमुपाशिक्षत् । तस्मै बह्वददात् ।

**English**

The king approached him in supplication and gave him abundant gifts.

## Passage 9

**Sanskrit**

सोऽग्नये सुरभिमतेऽष्टाकपालं निरवपच्छुक्लानां व्रीहीणाम् । ततो वै तद्व्युदस्यत् ।

**English**

Vaka then offered an eight-portion sacrificial cake of white rice to Agni Surabhimat, Agni in his auspicious, fragrant form. The affliction was thereby removed.

## Passage 10

**Sanskrit**

अग्नये रुद्रवतेऽष्टाकपालं निर्वपेत् कृष्णानां व्रीहीणामभिचरन् ।

**English**

A person performing a hostile rite should offer an eight-portion cake of black rice to Agni Rudravat.

## Passage 11

**Sanskrit**

अग्निर्वै रुद्रो रुद्रायैवैनमपिदधाति ।

**English**

Agni is indeed Rudra; thus the sacrificer hands the target over to Rudra.

## Passage 12

**Sanskrit**

कृष्णानां व्रीहीणां भवति । कृष्णमिव वै तमः । तमो मृत्युः । मृत्युनैवैनं ग्रहयति ।

**English**

The offering consists of black rice. Darkness is, as it were, black; darkness is death. Thus he causes death to seize the target.

## Passage 13

**Sanskrit**

अग्नये सुरभिमतेऽष्टाकपालं निर्वपेद्यर्ह्ययं देवः प्रजा अभिमन्येत, यदा कामयेत विदस्येदिति ।

**English**

When this deity turns in anger against living beings, and one wishes that the danger should disperse, an eight-portion cake should be offered to Agni Surabhimat.

## Passage 14

**Sanskrit**

एषा वा अग्नेर्भिषज्या तनूर्या सुरभिमती । तामेव भागधेयेनोपधावति । तयैना भिषज्यति ।

**English**

For the Surabhimat form is Agni’s healing manifestation. By offering him his allotted share, one approaches that very form; through it he heals those afflicted.

## Comparative reading
